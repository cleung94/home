Understanding the C•CURE 9000 Training Pathway

A practical guide to the Software House C•CURE 9000 Integrator training path—what each course teaches, when to take it, and how the certifications build toward supporting enterprise physical security systems.

October 2026 12 min read

One of the things I was recently tasked with was researching the next C•CURE 9000 training course I should take and making sure my certifications continue to align with the systems and responsibilities I support professionally.

I had already completed C•CURE 9000 Installer/Maintainer and Advanced Integrator training, so what initially sounded like a simple question—what class comes next?—turned into a deeper look at how the entire Software House Integrator training catalog is structured.

The catalog contains several C•CURE courses, but they do not all form one long certification ladder. Some represent the main progression of technical knowledge, while others are specialized courses intended for specific products or deployment types.

The C•CURE training path makes more sense when you separate the core technical progression from the specialized courses you take based on the environment you actually support.

About this guide

This article is an independent overview based on the publicly available Software House / Johnson Controls Integrator training catalog reviewed in October 2026. Course schedules, pricing, prerequisites, and availability can change, so current course information should always be verified before registration.

The C•CURE 9000 Integrator Training Catalog

The current Integrator catalog lists six C•CURE-specific technician courses:

  • AC9001 – C•CURE 9000 Installer Maintainer Certification
  • AC9003 – C•CURE IQ Installer Course for Integrators
  • AC9005 – C•CURE 9000 Advanced Integrator Certification
  • AC9444 – C•CURE 9000 Enterprise Architecture Certification
  • AC9201 – C•CURE 9000 Visitor Management Certification
  • AC9202 – C•CURE 9000 High Assurance with Innometriks

However, if the goal is to understand the primary technical certification progression, three courses stand out:

Level 1 AC9001 Installer / Maintainer
→
Level 2 AC9005 Advanced Integrator
→
Level 3 AC9444 Enterprise Architecture

The Core Integrator Pathway

The following three courses provide the clearest progression from learning how to install and maintain an individual C•CURE 9000 system to understanding how multiple C•CURE application servers operate together in an enterprise environment.

1
AC9001 • LEVEL 1

C•CURE 9000 System Installer/Maintainer

Foundation

AC9001 is the foundation of the technical Integrator pathway. The course is designed to teach Integrator technicians how to install, configure, and maintain a C•CURE 9000 server and client system.

Cost $2,055 USD MSRP
Duration 4 Days*
Certification Level 1

What the course covers:

  • C•CURE 9000 installation and licensing
  • Software upgrades
  • Server Configuration Application
  • Administration Station and Monitoring Station
  • Hardware architecture
  • iSTAR controllers
  • Clusters and controllers
  • Expansion modules and readers
  • I/8 and R8 boards
  • RM readers
  • Door access configuration
  • Card formats and personnel
  • Events and alarm automation
  • Maps
  • Holidays and schedules
  • Groups and Dynamic Views
  • Queries and reports
  • Operators and privileges
  • Log management
  • Database management
  • Backup and restore
  • Badging

Who is it for?
Field installation and service technicians who need to install, configure, troubleshoot, and maintain C•CURE 9000 systems.

When should you take it?
This is the starting point if you are entering the technical C•CURE Integrator pathway. It establishes the foundation required before moving into Advanced Integrator.

Prerequisites:
Familiarity with Windows, TCP/IP networking, basic access-control concepts, and low-voltage electronic devices such as controllers and readers. Basic SQL knowledge is recommended but not required.

*The official course page states that the previous five-day course moves to a four-day format beginning with October 2026 sessions.

AC9005 • LEVEL 2

C•CURE 9000 Advanced Integrator

Advanced

AC9005 picks up where Installer/Maintainer ends. It is designed for senior technical personnel involved in the design and implementation of security systems and moves deeper into advanced administration, reporting, video integration, and troubleshooting.

Cost Check Current Session
Duration 5 Days
Certification Level 2

What the course covers:

  • Advanced C•CURE 9000 administration
  • Keypad commands
  • Intrusion Zones
  • Partitioning
  • Automated import
  • Web Client
  • Video server integration concepts
  • IP cameras
  • CCTV switchers
  • Video Tours and Views
  • Detailed queries and reports
  • Advanced troubleshooting techniques

Who is it for?
Senior personnel responsible for designing and implementing end-user security systems and personnel who supervise or support Installer/Maintainer-level technicians.

When should you take it?
Take Advanced Integrator after you are comfortable with the fundamentals of installing and maintaining C•CURE 9000 and your responsibilities are moving toward more complex configuration, design, administration, or technical troubleshooting.

Prerequisites:
Successful completion of AC9001, experience with access control and Windows, and approval as a Software House Integrator.

3
AC9444 • LEVEL 3

C•CURE 9000 Enterprise Architecture

My Next Step

AC9444 moves the training path from advanced administration into enterprise system architecture. C•CURE 9000 Enterprise Architecture allows multiple C•CURE servers to communicate with a Master Application Server.

The Master Application Server, or MAS, provides a platform for global management of personnel, video, and access-security objects across one or more Satellite Application Servers, or SAS.

Cost Check Current Session
Duration 2 Days
Certification Integrator Level 3

What the course covers:

  • MAS/SAS Enterprise theory of operation
  • Preparing MAS and SAS systems for installation
  • Installing Windows Server on the MAS
  • Installing SQL Server on the MAS
  • Installing C•CURE 9000 on the MAS
  • Installing C•CURE 9000 and SQL on SAS systems
  • MAS and SAS configuration
  • MAS and SAS operation
  • Enterprise synchronization
  • Synchronization conflict resolution
  • Server and network communication concepts

Who is it for?
Technical partners who design, install, configure, and maintain C•CURE Enterprise Architecture environments. The course is specifically focused on application servers and network communications rather than end-user operator functions.

When should you take it?
This is the logical next step when your responsibilities extend beyond an individual C•CURE server and into multi-server enterprise environments. It is particularly relevant when you need to understand MAS/SAS design, global objects, synchronization, SQL, and the communication between enterprise application servers.

Prerequisites:
Successful completion of C•CURE 9000 Advanced Integrator or C•CURE 9000 Advanced System Manager, Microsoft Networking Essentials or equivalent knowledge, and familiarity with installing SQL.

The Specialized C•CURE Courses

The other C•CURE courses in the Integrator catalog are better understood as specialized branches. They are valuable, but they are not simply Level 4, Level 5, and Level 6 after Enterprise Architecture.

Which one makes sense depends on the technology deployed in the environment you support.

So, What Should You Take and When?

Looking at the catalog as a whole, I would not approach it as a checklist where every technician needs to complete every course.

Instead, the training should follow the type of work you are actually responsible for.

Start with AC9001

If you are entering the technical Integrator path, AC9001 gives you the foundation. You need to understand the application, hardware, controllers, readers, personnel, events, schedules, privileges, logs, and database fundamentals before advanced topics become meaningful.

Move to AC9005 as Your Responsibility Grows

Advanced Integrator makes sense when you are no longer only installing or maintaining equipment. If you are supporting system design, advanced administration, integrations, reporting, or more complicated troubleshooting, the Level 2 material becomes much more relevant.

Take AC9444 When Your Scope Becomes Enterprise

Enterprise Architecture becomes important when one C•CURE application server is no longer the entire environment.

Once multiple SAS systems, a MAS, SQL, server communications, global objects, and synchronization are involved, understanding the architecture becomes just as important as understanding individual C•CURE objects.

Add Specialized Training When the Technology Requires It

If your organization deploys C•CURE IQ, Visitor Management, or High Assurance, those specialized courses become relevant based on the technologies you actually support.

That is a more practical approach than collecting certifications without having an environment where the knowledge can be applied.

Why Enterprise Architecture Is My Next Step

For my own path, this research answered the question I originally set out to solve.

I have already completed both the C•CURE 9000 Installer/Maintainer and Advanced Integrator training. My professional experience has also increasingly involved enterprise physical security environments rather than isolated single-site systems.

That makes AC9444 Enterprise Architecture the most logical next step.

The areas I am particularly interested in are the MAS/SAS architecture, synchronization, conflict resolution, SQL, and server communication portions of the course.

Those concepts answer a different category of question than learning how to configure an individual reader, event, schedule, or privilege.

The next stage of learning is not only knowing how to configure C•CURE. It is understanding why an enterprise C•CURE environment is architected the way it is and how changes move throughout that architecture.

Certification Expiration Is Also Changing

Another important part of the current Integrator training page is the introduction of certification expiration dates beginning in 2028.

  • C•CURE 9000 certifications obtained before July 1, 2025 will expire July 1, 2028.
  • C•CURE 9000 certifications obtained after July 1, 2025 will expire three years after their issue date.
  • Software House states that its recertification process will be announced closer to the July 1, 2028 expiration date.

This means certification planning is becoming an ongoing part of maintaining technical readiness rather than something that can simply be completed once and forgotten.

A Simple Way to Remember the Path

AC9001 Learn the System Install & Maintain
→
AC9005 Go Deeper Advanced Integration
→
AC9444 Think Enterprise MAS / SAS Architecture

From there, specialized courses can be added according to the environment:

  • AC9003: when supporting C•CURE IQ.
  • AC9201: when supporting Visitor Management, Web Portal, or Kiosk deployments.
  • AC9202: when supporting High Assurance, PIV/CAC, smart-card, or FICAM environments.

Key Takeaways

  • AC9001 is the Level 1 foundation of the C•CURE 9000 Integrator pathway.
  • AC9005 is the Level 2 Advanced Integrator course and requires AC9001.
  • AC9444 is the Level 3 Enterprise Architecture course and follows Advanced Integrator or Advanced System Manager.
  • The clearest core technical progression is AC9001 → AC9005 → AC9444.
  • AC9003, AC9201, and AC9202 are better understood as specialized branches based on the technology being deployed.
  • AC9001 currently lists an MSRP of $2,055.
  • AC9003 and AC9202 currently list prices of $1,620.
  • The currently accessible course pages for AC9005, AC9444, and AC9201 do not publish a course price, so current session pricing should be verified before registration.
  • C•CURE certifications are moving to an expiration model, making certification tracking and recertification increasingly important.
  • For my own training path, AC9444 Enterprise Architecture is the logical next step.
